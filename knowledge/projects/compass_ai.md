# Compass AI Platform

## Context

Recurly needed a scalable AI foundation to support multiple AI-powered features across its product suite. As enterprise customers increasingly expected AI capabilities, the company faced a choice between fragmented point solutions or a unified platform approach.

## Problem

Early AI efforts risked becoming fragmented, unsafe, and difficult to govern at enterprise scale. Without a centralized platform, each AI initiative would need to solve fundamental problems like data access, security, and permissions independently.

### Key Challenges

- Ensuring safe access to sensitive billing and subscription data
- Maintaining compliance with enterprise security requirements
- Avoiding duplication of AI infrastructure across teams
- Establishing governance for AI features
- Enabling rapid development without compromising safety

## Approach

Jordan led the design of Compass as a centralized AI platform layer. The system focused on:

### Secure Data Access

Designed controlled, auditable access patterns for AI agents to interact with billing and subscription data.

### Reusable Tooling

Built a library of tools and capabilities that AI agents could use, avoiding reimplementation across features.

### Clear Boundaries

Established clear separation between models, tools, and permissions, allowing independent evolution of each component.

### Enterprise Standards

Incorporated compliance, security, and governance requirements from the initial design.

## Outcome

The Compass platform delivered measurable impact:

**Infrastructure Efficiency**: Enabled multiple AI initiatives without duplicating infrastructure, reducing development overhead.

**Development Velocity**: Improved speed of developing AI features by providing ready-to-use platform capabilities.

**Strategic Alignment**: Established a long-term AI strategy aligned with enterprise customer needs and compliance requirements.
