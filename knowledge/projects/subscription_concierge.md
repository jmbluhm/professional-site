# Subscription Concierge

## Context

Subscription customers needed better, more automated ways to manage changes, issues, and questions. Traditional support channels created bottlenecks, and customers expected self-service capabilities for common subscription management tasks.

## Problem

Manual support and rigid flows created friction and inefficiency. Support teams handled repetitive requests that could be automated, while customers faced delays in resolving simple issues like subscription modifications, payment updates, or order changes.

### Customer Pain Points

- Long wait times for simple subscription changes
- Limited self-service options
- Inconsistent experience across channels
- Repetitive manual work for support teams

## Approach

Jordan helped launch an AI-powered subscription concierge capable of handling common customer interactions across channels. The system used natural language understanding to interpret customer requests and agent-based workflows to execute changes safely.

### Key Capabilities

- Natural language interaction for subscription management
- Automated handling of common requests
- Multi-channel support
- Safe execution of subscription modifications
- Escalation to human support when needed

## Outcome

The Subscription Concierge delivered tangible results:

**Support Efficiency**: Reduced support burden by automating common subscription management tasks.

**Customer Experience**: Improved customer experience through faster resolution times and increased self-service options.

**Production AI**: Demonstrated early agentic workflows in production, validating the approach for future AI features.

## Technical Innovation

This project represented an early example of production AI agents handling sensitive customer operations. It established patterns for safe AI-driven workflows that informed later platform development.
