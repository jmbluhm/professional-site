# Operating in Ambiguity

## Core Capability

Jordan is comfortable working in areas where requirements are unclear and constraints evolve. He treats ambiguity as a normal condition of product development rather than a problem to be eliminated.

## Approach to Uncertainty

### Creating Clarity Through Iteration

Rather than attempting to resolve all ambiguity upfront, Jordan focuses on creating clarity progressively through small iterations and validated learning.

### Progressive Refinement

He starts with rough direction and refines it based on feedback, data, and changing circumstances. This allows forward progress even when complete information is unavailable.

## Stakeholder Alignment

### Principle-Based Alignment

When specific requirements are unclear, Jordan aligns stakeholders on principles and constraints. This provides guidance for decisions without requiring premature commitment to details.

### Shared Understanding

He ensures stakeholders understand the sources of uncertainty and agree on how decisions will be made as new information becomes available.

### Managing Expectations

Jordan sets appropriate expectations about what can be known at different stages, preventing stakeholders from expecting certainty where it does not exist.

## Incremental Shipping

### Reducing Risk Through Iteration

Shipping incrementally allows learning from real usage rather than speculation. Each increment provides information that reduces uncertainty for subsequent work.

### Adaptive Planning

Plans adapt based on what is learned from each increment. This approach acknowledges that initial assumptions may be wrong and allows course correction.

## Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

### Reversible vs. Irreversible Decisions

Jordan distinguishes between decisions that are easily reversed and those that are not. He moves quickly on reversible decisions while taking more time with irreversible ones.

### Information Gathering

He gathers information proportional to decision importance and reversibility, avoiding both analysis paralysis and reckless commitment.

## Application to New Domains

This capability is particularly valuable in emerging areas like AI product development, where best practices are still evolving and requirements often emerge through experimentation.
