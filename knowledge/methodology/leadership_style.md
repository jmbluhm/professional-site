# Leadership and Collaboration Style

## Cross-Functional Collaboration

Jordan works closely with engineering, design, and data teams. His leadership style emphasizes partnership rather than directive management.

## Core Values

### Clear Communication

Jordan prioritizes clear, direct communication that eliminates ambiguity and ensures shared understanding across teams.

### Technical Communication

He communicates technical concepts to business stakeholders and business requirements to technical teams, ensuring alignment without translation loss.

### Transparent Decision-Making

Decisions and their rationale are communicated clearly, allowing teams to understand context and constraints.

## Team Partnerships

### Engineering Collaboration

Jordan builds strong partnerships with engineering teams, respecting technical constraints while advocating for user needs. He engages in technical discussions and understands architectural implications of product decisions.

### Design Partnership

He collaborates closely with design teams on user experience, balancing ideal experiences with technical and business constraints.

### Data Team Coordination

Jordan works with data teams to ensure products are instrumented properly and that decisions are informed by evidence.

## Mutual Respect

### Discipline Expertise

He respects the expertise of each discipline and does not assume product management has all answers. He views product development as a collaborative effort drawing on diverse perspectives.

### Trust and Autonomy

Jordan provides context and direction while giving teams autonomy to determine implementation details within agreed constraints.

## Translation Skills

### Multi-Perspective Communication

Jordan translates between business, technical, and user perspectives, ensuring that business goals, technical realities, and user needs are all represented in decisions.

### Conflict Resolution

When perspectives conflict, he facilitates resolution by making tradeoffs explicit and ensuring all stakeholders understand the implications of different approaches.

## Influence Without Authority

Jordan influences through clear reasoning, data, and building consensus rather than relying on positional authority. This approach is effective in cross-functional environments where product managers rarely have direct authority over other functions.
