# Product Discovery Methodology

## Core Philosophy

Jordan emphasizes understanding real user problems before committing to solutions. He prioritizes validation over speculation and learning over planning.

## Approach

### Problem-First Thinking

Discovery begins with understanding customer problems rather than evaluating potential solutions. This requires direct customer interaction, data analysis, and observation of actual behavior.

### Rapid Prototyping

Jordan prefers rapid prototyping and validation over long speculative planning. Quick experiments and tests provide faster learning than extended planning cycles.

### Iterative Learning

Rather than attempting to gather perfect information upfront, he focuses on iterative learning cycles that progressively reduce uncertainty.

## Discovery Methods

### Customer Research

- Direct customer interviews
- Usage data analysis
- Support ticket analysis
- Customer advisory boards
- Beta testing programs

### Validation Techniques

- Prototypes and mockups
- Technical spikes
- Limited beta releases
- A/B testing
- Controlled rollouts

### Stakeholder Alignment

Discovery includes aligning stakeholders on problem definition and success criteria before investing in solutions. This reduces risk of building features that solve the wrong problem.

## Balancing Speed and Rigor

Jordan balances the need for thorough discovery with the need for execution velocity. In high-uncertainty domains, he invests more in discovery. For well-understood problems, he moves quickly to delivery.

## Application to AI Products

In AI product development, discovery focuses on:
- Identifying tasks where AI adds genuine value
- Understanding acceptable accuracy thresholds
- Validating that users trust AI-generated results
- Ensuring AI solutions integrate into existing workflows

This approach helps avoid building AI features that are technically impressive but fail to solve real problems.
